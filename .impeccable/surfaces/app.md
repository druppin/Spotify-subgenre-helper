# Surface: the app (Subgenre sorter, My library, Settings)

Mode: operate

## Direction contract

THESIS: Filing a song should feel like dropping a record behind the right divider. Refuses the streaming-app default of near-black panels with one green accent, and the grey SaaS dashboard.

OWN-WORLD: Record shop at closing time. Stained-wood ground (#1A1613), sleeve-paper ink (#F1E9DD), an oxblood shell band (#5E1A24), genre-family colours printed like divider card stock (teal house/techno, violet bass, coral rock/punk/metal, mustard pop/disco/soul, blue hip hop, sage roots). Genre colour lives only on divider tabs, tag dots and confidence bars. Archivo, narrow width for titles and tabs. A light mode keeps the same world in daylight (sleeve-paper ground, wood-ink text, the same oxblood band). The bright action colour appears only on pressable filing actions.

STORY: The listener plays through a pile, sees how sure the AI is of each tag, and files the song behind one or more dividers without losing their place.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Today's three panels. Left: source playlist with an exact-length confidence bar and % per song. Centre: cover, title, transport, the AI summary card with tags and chat. Right: "File it under", with starred playlists as crate dividers (cover, genre-coloured staggered tab, top genres, Add) above a dense searchable list of every other playlist.

FORM: Record shop crate, position 4 of 7 on the grounded list. seed:d7566a3141689b7c
